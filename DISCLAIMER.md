## What this tool is

mychart-advisor helps patients and families be better informed about their own medical records. It helps them understand what test results and clinical notes mean, follow trends over time, see the possibilities the evidence supports and which tests would settle them, and prepare sharper questions for the care team. It also answers the questions a tool can answer, so the family's limited time with doctors and nurses goes to the questions only those clinicians can answer.

## What this tool is not

It does not diagnose and it does not direct care. The treating team makes the diagnosis and every treatment decision. Possible diagnoses the tool discusses are hypotheses to understand and raise with the care team. None is a finding of fact. The tool never tells anyone to start, stop, or change a treatment or dose, and it never contacts anyone.

It is not medical advice. It is not a substitute for a licensed clinician who knows the patient. AI systems make mistakes, including wrong numbers, wrong dates, and wrong reasoning, even with the verification steps built in. Check anything important against the original record and with the care team.

**If you think the patient is having an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number, or alert the care team now. Do not wait for this tool.**

## Privacy

The plugin keeps records in a local folder you choose and instructs agents not to put identifiers into web searches. The AI model you run it with still receives whatever content it reads, under that provider's terms. Patient-held data given to a consumer AI tool is generally not protected by HIPAA. Decide what you are comfortable sharing with your AI provider before you start.
