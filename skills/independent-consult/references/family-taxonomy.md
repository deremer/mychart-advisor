# Default hypothesis families

A coverage checklist for triage and investigation, not a fixed list. Adapt it to the case. Split a family when it holds more than about twelve hypotheses. Merge small families when the preset has fewer investigators.

1. Neoplastic: primary, metastatic, hematologic, paraneoplastic
2. Infectious, split when warranted into bacterial and mycobacterial, fungal, viral, and parasitic
3. Inflammatory, autoimmune, and granulomatous
4. Vascular: thrombotic, embolic, vasculitic, hemodynamic
5. Structural, mechanical, and hemorrhagic
6. Metabolic, toxic, endocrine, and nutritional, including supplements and environmental exposures
7. Degenerative
8. Genetic and congenital, including late-onset presentations
9. Iatrogenic effects, confounders, test artifacts, and two-disease explanations

Also generate hypotheses about the mechanism of the main symptom, such as swelling, pressure, a metabolic derangement, or a drug effect. The mechanism may be separate from the underlying cause and treatable on its own.
