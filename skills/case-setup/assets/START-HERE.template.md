# Start here

This folder keeps {{PATIENT_LABEL}}'s portal records organized so an AI assistant can help you understand them and prepare questions for the care team. It does not diagnose and it does not direct care.

**If you think this is an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number, or alert the care team now.**

## Where files go

| Download from the portal | Put it in |
|---|---|
| Each test result (labs, imaging, pathology, cultures), saved as PDF | `Test Results/` |
| Each clinical note (doctor, nurse, therapy, pharmacy), saved as PDF | `Care Team Notes/` |
| Medication lists, discharge papers, outside records, photos of paperwork | `Other Records/` |

Keep the file names the portal gives you. Duplicates are fine, and the tools sort them out.

## What to say to the assistant

| When | Say |
|---|---|
| You downloaded new files | "Ingest the new results." |
| You noticed something at the bedside, or a doctor told you something | "Record an observation." |
| Something big changed, or a decision is coming | "Run a case conference." |
| You are about to talk with the care team | "Prep questions for rounds tomorrow." |
| You want a fresh, fully independent look | "Run an independent consult." |
| You remembered more history | "Add to the patient history." |

## What stays private

Everything in this folder stays on this computer, except what the AI model reads while it works, which your AI provider receives under its own terms. The assistant is told never to put names, dates of birth, record numbers, facilities, or clinician names into web searches.
